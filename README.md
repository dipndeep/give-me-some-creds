## 📌 Project Overview & Business Framing

In retail lending, financial institutions face a severely **asymmetric risk structure**:

- **Net Margin**: Lending institutions earn 8% – 15% per annum on performing loans.
- **Loss on Default**: A borrower defaulting (90+ days past due) results in up to **100% loss of unpaid principal** plus legal and collection costs.
- **Economic Reality**: A single default can wipe out the interest profits generated by **8 to 12 performing borrowers**.

### The "93% Accuracy Trap"

On this benchmark dataset of 150,000 borrowers, the baseline default rate is **6.68% (10,026 defaulted vs 139,974 performing)**.
A naive model predicting _"No Default"_ for everyone achieves **93.32% accuracy**, yet fails to detect a single defaulted borrower—guaranteeing massive capital write-offs. Therefore, this project evaluates performance using **ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and Recall**, rather than raw accuracy.

## 📊 Dataset Information

- **Source**: [Kaggle — Give Me Some Credit Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/brycecf/give-me-some-credit-dataset)
- **Training Instances**: 150,000 borrower profiles
- **Inference Instances**: 101,503 records
- **Target Feature**: `SeriousDlqin2yrs` (Binary: 1 = Borrower experienced 90+ days past due delinquency within 2 years; 0 = Performing)
- **Key Predictors**:
  - `RevolvingUtilizationOfUnsecuredLines`: Credit balance divided by credit limit
  - `age`: Borrower age in years
  - `NumberOfTime30-59DaysPastDueNotWorse`: Frequency of 30–59 days delinquency
  - `DebtRatio`: Monthly debt payments divided by gross income
  - `MonthlyIncome`: Monthly gross income (USD)
  - `NumberOfOpenCreditLinesAndLoans`: Number of open credit lines
  - `NumberOfTimes90DaysLate`: Frequency of 90+ days past due delinquency
  - `NumberRealEstateLoansOrLines`: Mortgage and home equity loan count
  - `NumberOfTime60-89DaysPastDueNotWorse`: Frequency of 60–89 days delinquency
  - `NumberOfDependents`: Number of family dependents

## 🛠️ Data Cleaning & Hygiene Summary (Day 1)

| Issue / Feature          | Raw Data State                                      | Engineering & Cleaning Solution                | Engineered Indicator            |
| :----------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| **Class Imbalance**      | 93.32% Non-Default vs 6.68% Default                 | Metric realignment (ROC-AUC & Recall target)   | Target: `SeriousDlqin2yrs`      |
| **Age Anomaly**          | 1 record with `age = 0`                             | Imputed with training median age (52 years)    | —                               |
| **Bureau Error Codes**   | 269 records with values 96 & 98 in past-due columns | Isolated special status; recoded to 0          | `HasPastDueRecordError`         |
| **Missing Income**       | 29,731 records (19.82% missing)                     | Imputed with age-cohort medians (anti-leakage) | `MonthlyIncome_is_missing`      |
| **Utilization Outliers** | Extreme values up to 50,708.0                       | Capped at 99th percentile (winsorized)         | `RevolvingUtilizationOverLimit` |
| **Extreme Debt Ratio**   | Outliers up to 329,664.0 (income=0 anomaly)         | Capped at 99th percentile                      | `DebtRatio_is_extreme`          |
| **Missing Dependents**   | 3,924 records (2.62% missing)                       | Imputed with mode/median (0 dependents)        | `NumberOfDependents_is_missing` |
